The portal should feel like a 1970s records bureau that never closed. Game screens are thin orange line-work on near-black, like a field terminal. The home screen is a cream CRT on a walnut wall under a honeycomb ceiling. Anything official is typed on aged paper and stamped. Every mark on this page, including the lettering and the seal, is drawn from scratch for Marvelous. No official logos, characters, photos or stills are used.

For review
Form 02

Palette

Burnt orange carries every frame and every exact match. Mustard means close. Chocolate means no. Teal appears only as a hairline, never as a fill.

Measured contrast on the void ground: cream 15.2:1 · orange 6.2:1 · muted text 7.8:1 · dark ink on orange 5.4:1 · on mustard 7.3:1. Orange on paper is 2.2:1, so orange lettering on paper is display-only and always has a plain-text equivalent.

Form 03

Lettering

One display face for the site name and game titles, one condensed mono for the terminal edges, one readable mono for play, one typewriter for anything on paper.

Marvelous Display. An original geometric stencil cut for this project: chunky round bowls, triangle joins, and two oranges per letter. It ships as our own web font under the SIL Open Font License, so it's licensed for web use from day one.
